---
title: Why the schedulers would not load
tags: [ebpf, sched_ext, kernel]
---

1. Built a sched_ext sandbox for the pathway report: a Lima VM on the M5 with a kernel that has `CONFIG_SCHED_CLASS_EXT=y`, the scx tree at `fe0407c`, and a smoke script that runs the same `stress-ng` load twice, once under EEVDF and once with a scheduler attached.

2. On kernel 7.0 `scx_lavd` attaches in about two seconds, holds, and detaches cleanly. `scx_bpfland` aborts, first on an arena read at an unmapped address and then on an EDQ insert. `scx_eevdf` refuses to load and says it needs 7.2.

3. Took two wrong turns before the answer. First clang: installed 22, cleared the build directory, and confirmed a real rebuild by watching the BPF program hash change from `31d90960` to `e17ad2b9`. The abort stayed, and only moved from the arena to the EDQ. Then modules: rebuilt the guest with `CONFIG_MODULES=n`, same failure.

4. The cause was the kernel. The arena and EDQ paths need sched_ext `cid` support that arrived in 7.2. Built `7.2.0-rc7` with four config changes: no modules, vsock, function tracer, and sched_ext with BTF. The first 7.2 boot came up healthy and invisible, because Lima reaches the guest over vsock and I had compiled it out.

5. All three schedulers pass on 7.2-rc7. Four repeats of `scx_lavd` against its own baseline came out -3.3%, -1.5%, -1.2% and +2.7%. The sign flips, so a single run here is noise.

6. Both failures were caught by the crash watchdog. Nothing reported that the policy was making a bad decision until it made an illegal one.
